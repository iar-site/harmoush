Professional Profile

A career shaped by faith, education, and community service.

Imam Mahmoud Harmoush is an Islamic religious leader, educator, speaker, and community advocate whose work has brought together religious service, higher education, family guidance, and interfaith engagement in Southern California.

He earned a graduate degree in Islamic Studies and Leadership from Claremont School of Theology. His teaching experience includes Arabic Language, the World of Islam, and Middle Eastern Culture and Art at California State University, San Bernardino.

His leadership experience includes serving as National President of the Muslim Students Association of the United States and Canada during his undergraduate studies at Cal Poly Pomona, as well as participation in the Shura Council of the Islamic Society of North America.

His public service has included religious instruction, Friday sermons, marriage and family counseling, new-Muslim support, university and community presentations, and interfaith dialogue. He has spoken at churches, universities, and civic forums on Islam, Muslim life, and understanding across faith traditions.

He was appointed to the City of Riverside Human Relations Commission for a four-year term, contributing to civic efforts focused on discrimination, inclusion, and mutual respect.

Today, his professional services draw on this breadth of experience to offer Nikah and matrimonial services, religious and social consultation, community speaking, and Friday khutbah engagements.

His approach emphasizes faith, thoughtful listening, education, compassion, and service.
